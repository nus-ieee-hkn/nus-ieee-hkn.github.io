  • NUS ECE Research Selected as ECCV 2026 Award Candidate

    Congratulations to ECE PhD student Mr. Zhang Shihua and his co-authors from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), National University of Singapore (NUS), whose paper, “Make Geometry Matter for Spatial Reasoning,” was selected as one of 10 Award Candidates at the 19th European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV 2026). The paper was also accepted as an ECCV 2026 Long Oral Presentation. Mr. Zhang Shihua is supervised by Associate Professor Wang Xinchao. The paper was co-authored by Shihua Zhang, Qiuhong Shen, Shizun Wang, Tianbo Pan and Xinchao Wang.

  • NUS ECE Students Win Second Prize at Intel Cup 2026 with On-Device AI Assistant for Visually Impaired Users

    A team of NUS students has won Second Prize at the Intel Cup 2026 Undergraduate Embedded System Design Competition, held in Shanghai in July 2026, for their project EdgeSight, an on-device visual-language assistant designed to support visually impaired users. The team comprises Ms. Badrinath Sandhya (Year 3, Electrical Engineering), Mr. Chan Zun Mun Terence (Year 4, Computer Engineering), and Ms. Hsu Myat Noe (Year 3, Computer Science). The project was supervised by Dr Rajesh Panicker from the NUS Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE).
